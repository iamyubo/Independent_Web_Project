The color, yellow, evokes strong, contrasting feelings of joy, warmth, and optimism, alongside potential frustration or anxiety if it is too intense. As the most visible color to the human eye, it grabs attention quickly, stimulates mental clarity, and increases alertness.

The furniture store, IKEA, uses yellow because it helps create a strong, inviting visual contrast—making giant blue-and-yellow warehouse stores and signs easily recognizable from a distance. hroughout its stores and website, yellow highlights special tags or low prices to catch customer attention.
